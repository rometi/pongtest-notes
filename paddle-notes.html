Pong paddle tuning notes

Community notes, revised 2026-10-19.

These notes collect what players and testers found when tuning paddle movement in two-player Pong clones.

Three rules

  1. Paddle speed. The paddle moves 6 pixels per frame at 60 frames per second. Testers found 8 pixels per frame too fast to control and 4 too slow to reach a fast ball.
  2. Ball acceleration. The ball speeds up by 2% at each paddle hit, up to twice its starting speed. Beyond that limit, rallies end on reaction time rather than on skill.
  3. Hit angle. The bounce angle depends on where the ball hits the paddle: the centre sends it straight, the edges send it at up to 60 degrees.

Why 6 pixels

At 6 pixels per frame, a paddle crosses a 480-pixel playfield in 80 frames, about 1.3 seconds, which matches the time a ball at starting speed takes to cross the field. The two timings being equal is what makes the first rally fair.
